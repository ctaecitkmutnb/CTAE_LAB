Welcome to the CTAE Lab

Automotive Engineering Technology (AmET), College of Industrial Technology, KMUTNB

Focusing on the research and development of innovative combustion technologies and sustainable alternative energy solutions to meet the real-world demands of industry and society.

Research Areas

🌱 Carbon-Free & Alternative Fuels

Researching and developing zero-carbon energy sources, including hydrogen, ammonia, and advanced biofuels, for a sustainable future.

⚡ Nonthermal Plasma & Catalysis

Utilising innovative catalysts and nonthermal plasma technologies to enhance energy conversion and fuel reforming processes.

🌍 Decarbonisation & Emission Reduction

Developing cutting-edge solutions to mitigate greenhouse gas emissions and drive industrial decarbonisation towards net-zero targets.
